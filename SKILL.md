## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a private visual diary or half-remembered fragment. Keep one memorable instant, action or relation. Small subject, abundant whitespace, vignette-like and possibly cropped or off-centre. People, if present, stay unposed, slightly clumsy and anonymous in the face. Loose oil pastel and crayon: simplified forms, contours that do not fully close, broken edges, paper grain, waxy drag and uneven coverage. Stop before everything is described. Palette from the source temperature: warm ivory, soft blue-grey, plant green, terracotta, apricot brown, grey-pink, with one or two warm lights. Quiet sophisticated naïveté. It is not a finished illustration, a brown vintage filter, neon, candy sweetness or a commercial caption bar.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-240/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
